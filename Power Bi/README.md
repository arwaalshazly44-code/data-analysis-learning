### Practice 2=> student BI 
# Student Spending Dashboard

## Dataset

**Student Spending Habits Dataset**

[View Dataset on Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/sumanthnimmagadda/student-spending-dataset)

## Dataset Idea

The dataset contains information about students' demographics, income, financial aid, spending categories, and preferred payment methods.

The goal is to understand students' spending behavior and identify patterns that can support marketing decisions.

## Dashboard Scenario

Imagine a company that wants to launch a marketing campaign targeting university students.

The dashboard helps the marketing team understand:

* What students spend the most money on.
* Which student groups have higher spending.
* Which payment methods are most popular.
* Which audiences may be more relevant for targeted campaigns.

## Dashboard Information

The dashboard includes:

* Total Students
* Total Spending
* Average Monthly Income
* Average Financial Aid
* Average Spending per Student
* Spending by Category
* Spending by Year in School
* Spending by Major
* Spending by Gender
* Payment Method Distribution

## Key Insights

* **Tuition** has the highest spending, making education-related services and offers a potential area for marketing campaigns.

* **Psychology students** have the highest average spending per student, making them a potential high-value target audience.

* **Biology** has the highest total spending, partly because it has a larger student population.

* **Mobile Payment App** has the highest total spending and the largest user base, making it a strong channel for digital marketing campaigns.

* **Spending is relatively similar across academic years**, suggesting that campaigns may not need to focus heavily on a specific study year.

## Marketing Campaign Decision

Based on the dashboard insights, the marketing team could focus on **high-spending student segments** and **digital payment channels**.

Potential campaign opportunities include:

* Targeting **Psychology students** with personalized offers.
* Promoting **education-related products and services** due to high tuition spending.
* Using **mobile payment apps** as an important digital campaign channel.
* Considering **Biology students** as a large audience with high total spending.

The dashboard helps transform student spending data into **actionable marketing insights and audience targeting decisions**.
