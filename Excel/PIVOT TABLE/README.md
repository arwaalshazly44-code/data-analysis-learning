# Pivot Table Practice

This practice contains 17 business analysis questions solved using Excel Pivot Tables and related Excel functions.

## Questions

1. Revenue by Channel
2. Revenue by Category
3. Revenue by Region
4. Revenue by Month
5. Revenue by Promotion
6. Revenue by Manufacturer
7. Top Five Cities in Each Country by Revenue
8. Relation Between Cost and Revenue by Subcategory
9. Top Four Products in Each Subcategory
10. Profit by Quarter in Each Year
11. Relation Between Profit% and Cost for Top 10 Products
12. Relation Between Profit% and Quantity for Subcategory
13. Revenue% for Each Channel in Each Region
14. Month-over-Month Revenue% for Each Year
15. Rank Subcategories by Revenue, Profit, and Cost Within Each Category
16. Discount Based on Cost%
17. Revenue% for Each Subcategory Within Its Category
